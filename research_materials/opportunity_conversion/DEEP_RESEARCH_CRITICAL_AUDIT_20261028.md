## Verdict
The commissioned Deep Research response is a useful **breadth exercise and hypothesis catalog**, not an investable market report, verified opportunity shortlist or cost engineering study. It does **not** deliver 100–200 individually sourced SKU opportunities or 15–20 fully screened independent local comparisons; it describes categories, not validated cross-border spreads. ## Progression decision
Do not build a pet robot merely because Loona costs $429–499 or a sensor because an installed system sells for TWD 60k. Instead screen twelve independent cross-industry cases (next file), track five economics lanes, then test selected HS technical alternatives *against exact reference functions*.

### Engineering validation expectations
Use existing HS engineering landscape PR #227 and first hardware build challenge #224 as authority limits. Prior to physical verification, 'functional equivalence' must be tagged **not demonstrated**. Productive levers might be assembly from qualified existing modules, OEM sourcing, customization, integration, after-sales, or rental; these often beat bespoke engineering for commodity products.

### Commercial decision criteria
For a case to advance from RESEARCH to VALIDATION:
- actual buyer/job and a concrete reason incumbents are insufficient;
- independently confirmed existing alternatives in each target market, including variants;
- grounded SKU and performance comparison rather than similar-looking marketing;
- actual sourced quote (or explicitly marked preliminary published price), product-specific import/safety compliance path and full cost;
- HS or OEM delivery route with engineering evidence grade and service obligation;
- realistic buyer acquisition and non-purchased demand signal.

All findings are dated observed web marketing, published program listings or coverage, **not transaction receipts**. Every economic number in the research report remains illustrative unless independently sourced.
