## Source-scoped checks by field

### Battery Recycling Economics

- **B01 / entailed**: The study's techno-economic model reports potential recycling cost or profit between negative $21.43 and positive $21.91 per kWh. Scope: Results stem from a techno-economic simulation model rather than empirical balance sheets from operational recycling plants. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2021.102787)
- **B02 / overreach**: The authors experimentally verified that every commercially available battery pack can be recycled profitably. Scope: Empirical operational testing across all commercially active pack chemistries and configurations is not provided. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2021.102787)
- **B03 / overreach**: The study establishes the actual net profit of a planned recycling operation in Taiwan. Scope: Requires empirical financial and logistics data specific to a designated facility location and regulatory framework in Taiwan. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2021.102787)

### Classroom Ventilation Outcomes

- **C01 / entailed**: The Portuguese study assessed 1,019 students at 51 elementary schools and reported an association between elevated CO2 and lack of concentration. Scope: Observational cross-sectional design limited to elementary schools in Coimbra, Portugal without evaluating longitudinal or causal mechanisms. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1590/s1806-37132014000300009)
- **C02 / overreach**: The study proves that installing carbon-dioxide monitors in schools causally reduces asthma prevalence. Scope: Requires randomized interventional controlled trials testing school air monitors and ventilation improvements to determine effects on asthma incidence. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1590/s1806-37132014000300009)
- **C03 / contradicted**: The study reported statistically significant associations between elevated CO2 and asthma, rhinitis and wheezing. Scope: Evaluated within an urban setting with overall low ambient pollution levels, which the authors noted may limit detection of respiratory disease associations. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1590/s1806-37132014000300009)

### Dog Population Identity And Movement

- **D01 / entailed**: The three-year cohort study followed identified dogs in four communities across Bali and Johannesburg and found movement of dogs introduced by people. Scope: Findings are observational and restricted to four rabies-endemic communities in Indonesia and South Africa without active management interventions. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.12279)
- **D02 / overreach**: The study experimentally establishes that sterilization alone permanently reduces free-roaming dog populations in Taiwan. Scope: Requires controlled interventional trial data evaluating surgical sterilization outcomes specifically within free-roaming dog populations in Taiwan. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.12279)
- **D03 / contradicted**: The study found almost all identified free-roaming dogs were unowned and never fed by people. Scope: Applies to the specific surveyed communities where dogs were predominantly free-roaming but retained human ownership and care. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.12279)

### Ewaste Toxicant Control

- **E01 / entailed**: At three formal Swedish e-waste plants, personal air sampling found recycling workers had around ten to thirty times greater airborne exposure to most metals than office workers. Scope: The findings are restricted to 55 recycling workers and 10 office workers across three Swedish facilities without assessing long-term clinical health outcomes. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envint.2014.07.006)
- **E02 / overreach**: The study demonstrates that specific engineering ventilation and PPE interventions completely eliminate lead exposure at recycling plants. Scope: The abstract does not evaluate or quantify any engineering ventilation or PPE intervention trials. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envint.2014.07.006)
- **E03 / contradicted**: The office workers had approximately ten to thirty times greater airborne exposure to most metals than the recycling workers. Scope: Comparative baseline derived from a small sample of 10 office workers and 55 recycling workers in Sweden. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envint.2014.07.006)

### Flood Warning To Impact

- **FL01 / entailed**: This mixed-methods Uganda study sampled 939 respondents and characterized GKMA as primarily flash/pluvial exposed and Kasese as riverine flood exposed. Scope: Findings are based on cross-sectional self-reported survey data from GKMA and Kasese and may not generalize to other Ugandan urban centers. [Source](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9885849/v1)
- **FL02 / overreach**: The study demonstrates that a deployed community early-warning system causally prevented deaths in Uganda. Scope: Requires experimental or quasi-experimental longitudinal evaluation of an early-warning system specifically measuring averted mortality. [Source](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9885849/v1)
- **FL03 / contradicted**: The abstract describes Kasese as primarily affected by rapid flash/pluvial flooding and GKMA by longer riverine floods. Scope: Spatial flood hazard typologies are localized to specific catchments and urban topographies and cannot be inverted across locations. [Source](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9885849/v1)

### Food Redistribution Outcomes

- **F01 / entailed**: The comparative lifecycle study found net environmental benefits across all five impact categories in each of its eight modeled donation scenarios. Scope: Limited to eight specific modeled scenarios using 391.8 kg of redistributed food over two weeks as the functional unit. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods15040645)
- **F02 / overreach**: Every food donation program necessarily produces a net reduction in environmental impacts under real-world operations. Scope: Results reflect a comparative LCA model of specific supply chain configurations rather than universal empirical audits of all operating programs. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods15040645)
- **F03 / contradicted**: The authors found every modeled scenario increased environmental burdens across all five measured categories. Scope: Evaluated across five defined lifecycle impact categories under the paper's specific LCA functional unit. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods15040645)

### Gig Worker Algorithmic Harm

- **G01 / entailed**: The narrative review searched Chinese and English sources through March 2026 and included 47 eligible sources on health and occupational risks of Chinese food-delivery riders. Scope: The synthesis is restricted to bibliographic literature retrieved from PubMed, Web of Science, and CNKI through March 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1932962)
- **G02 / overreach**: A randomized controlled trial in the review conclusively demonstrated that changing algorithmic dispatch reduces delivery-rider injury rates. Scope: Evaluating this claim requires experimental or quasi-experimental trial data testing algorithm modifications against verified injury outcomes. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1932962)
- **G03 / contradicted**: The authors characterize the delivery-rider literature as having extensive longitudinal and intervention evidence, with little reliance on self-reported exposure measures. Scope: The source is an evidence synthesis indicating high reliance on cross-sectional, self-reported survey methodologies across existing literature. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1932962)

### Heat Warning To Protective Action

- **H01 / entailed**: The modeling study projected heatwave-related death burdens for 176 countries using climate and adaptation scenarios across the 2030s, 2050s and 2090s. Scope: The projections rely on mathematical modeling and historical exposure-response curves rather than observed prospective mortality counts. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106419)
- **H02 / overreach**: The study measured that a deployed heat-warning system prevented one million deaths in the past year. Scope: Empirical evaluation would require observational or interventional surveillance data measuring mortality reductions following heat-warning deployments. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106419)
- **H03 / contradicted**: The reported figures for the 2090s are observed historical death counts, without future scenario projections. Scope: Estimates represent future simulated outcomes under defined emission and adaptation pathways rather than recorded historical epidemiology. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106419)
