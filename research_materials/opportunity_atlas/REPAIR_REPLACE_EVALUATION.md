Reproduction uses pinned third-party source in a separate research scratch directory. Source modifications are TEMPORARY LOCAL TRIALS, not upstream patches or tested products.

## Battery: reproducible accounting repair

Batteryloop is not a functional replacement on these inputs: at the pinned revision its economics module is empty, no root license file was detected, and no equivalent gate-fee scenario was executed. Do not extrapolate to all of its future code.

## Flood: repair prevents nonfinite sorting interference

Local guard returns None for nonfinite station fractions. The highest-risk function selected the genuine high reading for 6 of 6 possible permutations of low, NaN and high in the **local candidate**. The original can incorrectly choose low first. Real warning accuracy, delivery and safety remain untested.

## CO2: parseable alternative is not drop-in replacement

The original YAML does not parse. RomainGehrig's two alternatives parse after recognizing ESPHome's valid secret tag but require SCD40/SCD4x rather than SCD30, and refer to missing font assets in the inspected clone. No root license file detected; no right to redistribute inferred. The MIT-licensed langchr86/homesensor has SCD30 C++ driver sources but is NOT an ESPHome configuration and was not compiled.

## Boundaries

No full operational integration, source-independent research validation, legal clearance for all assets, real buyer, or verified profit. Model agreement is not treated as a functional experiment.
