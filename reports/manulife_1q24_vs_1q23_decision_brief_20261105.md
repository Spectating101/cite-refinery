# Manulife: improving core earnings, falling net income

Issuer-backed research brief | Q1 2024 versus Q1 2023 | Analyst review pending

## What matters

Core earnings (non-GAAP) increased +14.6% on displayed nominal C\$ figures, while net income attributed to shareholders fell -38.4%. The numerical gap between them widened from C\$125m to C\$888m. This is a real difference in printed values, NOT an established causal explanation.

APE sales rose +17.7%, new business CSM +48.9%, and NBV +31.4%. Core expenses rose +4.2%. These signals justify reviewing the non-GAAP reconciliation and currency context, not a trading conclusion.
