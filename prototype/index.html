THE LOOP

Problems are maintained, not merely posted.

Observe realityNocturnal preserves conditions, sources, disputes and later outcomes. Diagnose the problemPublic-Good separates symptoms, bottlenecks, constraints and authority. Establish the frontierCite checks prior evidence; Refinery checks existing capabilities. Open contribution pathsPeople contribute by capability and interest, not merely credential or curriculum. Govern interventionsDesigns cross evidence, safety, integrity, rights, reversibility, test and external-authority gates before real-world handoff. Observe what happenedOutcomes update the problem and reusable evidence/capabilities return to the commons.
