How to read the map

Known practice

Established method, low uncertainty. Competent execution matters more than novelty.

Adaptive practice

An established/adaptable method must be fitted to context and constraints.

Empirical inquiry

The condition, mechanism, or effect is uncertain enough that trustworthy new evidence is required.

Research frontier

The relevant method or knowledge is itself novel/frontier.

Authority is separate

A licensed practitioner or institution can be required for low-uncertainty work. A research degree does not inherit that authority.

The default system routes are suggestions only. Nocturnal observes, Cite establishes evidence, Refinery builds/reuses capability, Public-Good reasons about bounded intervention, and competent external actors retain consequential authority.
