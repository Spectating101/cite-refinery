## Reproducible gates and Nocturnal handoff

- `examples/legal_codex_case_posture_010.json` retains seven source-attributed events across three distinct cases plus a separate external legal opinion.
- `src/cite_refinery/legal_codex_case_posture.py` rejects case mergers, post-trial docket swaps, invented official sources, inferred trial-document custody, falsely certified finality and expert reports represented as court holdings. It emits a candidate-only semantic packet and no publication authority.


**Limitation:** Official court appellate *index* results may be visible to public search engines while direct Nocturnal or CI HTTP retrieval receives 403. Indexed metadata and links alone are not archived original PDFs or an authenticated court register, and full court judgments must be obtained through authorized channels before page-anchored analysis.
