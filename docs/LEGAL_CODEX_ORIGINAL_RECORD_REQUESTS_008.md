## Reconciliation acceptance conditions

A finding can advance from "reported lead" to "appointment verified" **only when** a signed/authorized original copy or equally authenticated official register resolves the full identifier, actor, office scope, effective date and any amendment. A finding can advance to "transaction matched" **only when** the system logs or original signed order resolve a concrete purchase number, buyer, approver, supplier, product, price and timestamp. The **two** records must have a date- and scope-consistent link.

A 2026 trial judgment finding about a 2020 appointment is a *judicial determination* with its own evidentiary weight; it still does not magically supply an absent 2020 signature or order ID. Do not merge the 2020 SD school grant and the 2020 SD/SMP directorate e-catalogue actions without original identifiers.

**No requests have been submitted.** These are prepared, limited-scope, fact-checking requests; the official PPID may require a local identity document, forms, applicant details and proof of eligibility. Do not invent or transmit private applicant identifiers.
